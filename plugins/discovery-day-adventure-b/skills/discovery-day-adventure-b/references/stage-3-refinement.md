# Stage 3 - Refine

## Purpose and milestone

Purpose: use the one-off preview as evidence, then improve the planner's usefulness and usability.

Milestone: the attendee has reviewed at least one preview, requested or accepted meaningful improvements, and approved the final planner design.

## Collect focused feedback

Start with the stage label **Adventure B - Stage 3 of 4: Refine**. Ask the attendee to evaluate:

- **Coverage:** Are important meetings, deadlines, people, and preparation actions present?
- **Relevance:** Is anything noisy, overly broad, or missing?
- **Evidence:** Do links reach the correct emails, files, meetings, transcripts, and sites?
- **Actionability:** Is it clear what to do before each event?
- **Layout:** Can they scan the week quickly?
- **Accessibility:** Are controls, labels, color use, keyboard behavior, and text contrast usable?

Encourage concrete feedback tied to the Weekly Preparation Planner. Invite the attendee to choose up to three changes for this refinement pass. Screenshots are useful for layout problems.

## Offer proven refinement options

Offer only options that fit the attendee's needs. Useful possibilities include:

- a persistent bottom detail panel instead of modal pop-ups;
- a visible, keyboard-accessible separator that resizes the detail panel;
- collapsible overview, priority, preparation, people, and day sections;
- a People This Week section next to the timeline;
- attendee chips sorted by meeting count;
- multi-select attendee highlighting;
- compact colored attendee markers inside meetings rather than full-card recoloring;
- conflict and focus-time indicators;
- clearer separation of scheduled events from recommended planning blocks; and
- concise preparation checklists with source links.

Color must never be the only way information is conveyed. Preserve labels or an accessible legend.

## Iterate deliberately

Apply the requested changes, produce another preview when the change is material, and verify the changed behavior. Do not silently add features merely because they appeared in an earlier example.

Keep the recurring-task specification synchronized with the approved Weekly Preparation Planner. A successful visual refinement is incomplete if the scheduled prompt would recreate the older design.

## Stage close

Summarize:

- what changed;
- what was tested;
- any remaining limitation;
- the final Weekly Preparation Planner behavior; and
- the final task specification.

Ask the attendee to approve the final planner design. When approved, announce that Stage 3 is complete and direct them to the **3 Refine booth** to collect the Refine stamp on their Discovery Day stamp card. Remind them that the four core stamps qualify them for the raffle.
