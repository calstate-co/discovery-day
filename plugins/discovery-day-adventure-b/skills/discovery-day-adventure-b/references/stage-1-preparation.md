# Stage 1 - Prepare

## Purpose and milestone

Purpose: turn a general wish to "be ready for the week" into a precise, safe task specification.

Milestone: the attendee has explicitly approved a Preparation Brief covering the planner's goal, schedule, skip rule, source list, output, and delivery preference.

## Begin with the outcome

After the attendee acknowledges the adventure overview, use the stage label **Adventure B - Stage 1 of 4: Prepare**. Explain that preparing the design prevents the task from gathering too much information or producing a planner that is attractive but not useful.

Ask one simple question at a time:

1. **Need:** What do they want to know or do before the workweek begins?
2. **Week window:** Which days count as their workweek?
3. **Run time:** What day, time, and timezone should the task use?
4. **Absence behavior:** Should it skip a week for full-week vacation, any out-of-office entry, or another rule?
5. **Output:** What should the planner help them notice - preparation, conflicts, decisions, people, deadlines, focus time, or something else?
6. **Delivery:** Should the Weekly Preparation Planner appear in ChatGPT, as an email draft, in another connected destination, or as a combination?

Offer a concrete default when useful. The default goal is to start the workweek prepared and focused by seeing top priorities, meeting preparation, conflicts, decisions, people, deadlines, and suggested focus time in one actionable planner. Run it Monday at 8:00 a.m. in the attendee's personal timezone, review the upcoming Monday-Friday workweek, and skip a normal briefing during a materially overlapping vacation.

## Choose sources

Present Outlook Calendar, Outlook Email, Zoom, SharePoint, and OneDrive as the starting menu. Ask the attendee to keep, remove, or add sources.

Ask whether each selected source is already connected to ChatGPT. Record the answer as connected, unconnected, or unknown. If a source is unconnected or the attendee is unsure, explain briefly that the Implement stage will check it and walk them through the available connection or approval steps. Do not require connections to be completed during Prepare.

For each chosen source, establish purpose and scope:

| Source | Suggested contribution | Scope question |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Outlook Calendar | Upcoming meetings, attendees, conflicts, locations, out-of-office | Which calendars and date range? |
| Outlook Email | Related context, commitments, decisions, and follow-ups | Inbox and Sent Items, or narrower folders? |
| Zoom | Prior-week summaries, transcripts, recordings, documents, or whiteboards | Which meetings and which available assets? |
| SharePoint | Project pages, plans, decision records, and reference material | Which sites or libraries? |
| OneDrive | Personal or shared working files | Which folders or named files? |

Accept other available apps or uploaded material. Do not assume every attendee uses all five suggested sources.

## Define boundaries

Confirm that discovery is read-only. Ask whether any topics, folders, sites, meeting types, people, or confidential material should be excluded. Keep sensitive-source scope narrow and purposeful.

Clarify that a one-off preview will be built before the recurring task is activated.

## Define the first output

Use the weekly planner blueprint as a menu rather than a mandatory design. At minimum, recommend:

- a concise week overview;
- top priorities and conflicts;
- preparation actions;
- a day-by-day timeline;
- related source links when available; and
- a clear distinction between scheduled events and suggested planning time.

Capture optional interactions separately so the first version remains achievable.

## Stage close

Summarize a Preparation Brief containing:

- goal;
- schedule and timezone;
- workweek window;
- absence skip rule;
- selected sources and scopes;
- known connection readiness;
- exclusions;
- output format and key sections;
- delivery choice; and
- permission boundary.

Ask the attendee to correct the brief. When it is correct, ask for explicit approval, such as: **"I approve this Preparation Brief."** Do not begin Implement until it is approved. When approved, announce that Stage 1 is complete and direct them to the **1 Prepare booth** to collect the Prepare stamp on their Discovery Day stamp card. Remind them that the four core stamps qualify them for the raffle.
