# Stage 1 - Prepare

## Purpose and milestone

Purpose: define the need, audience, purpose, content, visual direction, scope, and Site Brief for a manageable site that can be completed and published privately within the workspace during Discovery Day.

Milestone: the attendee has approved a Site Brief with a focused audience, purpose, content plan, visual direction, and scope boundary.

## Start with a small, finishable idea

Use the stage label **Adventure A - Stage 1 of 4: Prepare**. Explain that the goal is one focused page for one audience with one main purpose, not a complete app.

Invite Voice naturally: **"Want to talk it out? You can use Voice and tell me what you're imagining, or type your answer here."** If they use Voice, listen for decisions and turn them into a written brief for confirmation. Do not require Voice or imply that it was used when it was not.

Offer inspiration only when useful: a tiny event guide, a personal now-and-next page, a club or team resource hub, an interactive explainer, a local recommendation page, a project landing page, or a lightweight tracker/calculator.

Ask in small groups:

1. Who is the page for?
2. What should a visitor understand or do after spending a minute with it?
3. What is the page about, in one sentence?
4. Which three to five sections would make that happen?
5. What visual feeling fits it: calm, playful, bold, polished, nostalgic, or another direction?

Recommend a scope boundary: one page, three to five sections, one primary action, and no login, database, payments, or external integrations in the first version.

## Create and confirm the Site Brief

Write a concise Site Brief covering audience, one-sentence concept, primary visitor action, sections, content/assets available, visual direction, and scope boundary. Ask the attendee to correct or approve it.

## Stage close

Summarize the confirmed decisions and remaining gaps, state whether the Site Brief milestone is complete, and ask whether the attendee wants to continue unless they have already clearly asked to proceed. When complete, announce Stage 1 complete and direct the attendee to the **1 Prepare booth** to collect the Prepare stamp. Explain that the four core stamps qualify them for the raffle. For access or troubleshooting, direct them to the **IT Support booth in Anacapa**.
